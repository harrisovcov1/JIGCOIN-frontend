      One payment, with no automatic renewal. Your 30 days start after confirmed payment. Renewals add 30 days after your existing VIP expiry.

      • Twice your normal energy capacity, including active streak capacity bonuses. Regeneration speed stays the same; activation does not instantly fill the extra capacity.
      • Twice your normal tap coins, including active streak bonuses. The temporary 2× ad boost stacks with VIP.
      • Joining, referral, daily and fixed ad rewards, and purchased coin packs, keep their stated amounts. VIP does not multiply your existing balance.
      • When VIP expires, normal capacity and tap rewards return. Earned coins stay in your account.

      Fractional tap coins accumulate until they make a whole coin. The VIP bonus total counts extra coins actually credited since this update.

      VIP grants game benefits, not a cash return or guaranteed airdrop. For payment problems or refund requests, contact payment support below. A refunded pass loses its unused VIP time; separately purchased time and earned coins are preserved.
